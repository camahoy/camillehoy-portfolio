Research Automation

Crosstab Studio

Built a Streamlit app that ingests raw banner/crosstab Excel files from any research format and exports clean, publication-ready tables. Solved the problem of manual reformatting — hours of copying data out of SPSS-style banners into presentable output. Handles multiple format profiles (KP, IData, Corporate Reputation, Global Brand Identity), multi-wave comparison across files, and exports to Excel and Word.

Turns hours of manual reformatting into a single upload and click.
